Our Story

The Perch was created to provide visitors to Abuja with a peaceful, comfortable and private place to rest, work and reconnect.

The Perch Experience

Every space is prepared to offer the convenience of home with the thoughtful service of a premium hospitality property. From arrival to departure, our team quietly attends to the details so your stay feels effortless.

Hospitality Values

We hold ourselves to a standard of cleanliness, discretion and genuine care. Housekeeping, maintenance and guest support are managed closely so that every apartment is ready to receive you.

Location & Accessibility

Set within the secure, well-planned River Park Estate in Lugbe, The Perch is a short drive from the Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport — convenient for arriving guests and easy to reach for local visitors.

Safety, Comfort & Property Policies

Secure Estate

Gated, guarded residential estate with controlled access.

Reliable Power & Water

Backup power and water supply maintained across the property.

Housekeeping Standards

Professional cleaning between every stay.
